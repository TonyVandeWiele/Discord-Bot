# 📢 Patch Notes v1.1 - The Economy Update

**Tony** has been working hard in the lab. Here comes a massive update to the Defooz Economy!

## ⛏️ Crypto Mining
*The future is digital.*
- **New Command**: `/crypto shop` - Buy Racks, GPUs, and CPUs to build your mining rig.
- **Real-Time Prices**: We now fetch the **REAL** Bitcoin price. If BTC crashes in real life, your portfolio cries too.
- **Passive Income**: Your rig mines BTC every minute. Check stats with `/crypto stats` and sell with `/crypto sell`.

## 💸 Loans & Taxes
*Nothing is certain except death and taxes.*
- **Tax System**: Any income transaction over **1,000 Coins** is now taxed at **10%**. Proceeds go to the Global Jackpot.
- **Loan System**: broke? Ask a friend.
    - `/loan @user <amount> <interest>`: Offer a loan.
    - `/payloan <id>`: Repay your debts (or get your kneecaps broken... metaphorically).
    - `/loans`: View your active debts/credits.

## 🌧️ Make It Rain
*Success is best shared.*
- **Rain Command**: Use `/rain <amount>` to drop coins! A button appears, and users have **60 seconds** to join the pool. The total is split among active participants.
- **Casino Redistribution**: If you win big (>1500 coins) in Slots, you now get a button to **Share 20%** with the community. Be a hero.

## ⚖️ Balance Changes
- **Slots**: The Jackpot (7-7-7) probability has been reduced by **50%**.
- **Betting Tiers**: You can now bet **50, 500, or 5000 Coins** in Slots! High rollers welcome.
- **RTP Tuning**: Payouts have been adjusted to multipliers. Triple Win is now **15x** (was ~16x) and Double Win is **2x** to ensure long-term house profitability (~94% RTP).
- **Economy Adjustment**: 
    - **Passive Income**: Generator rates (Lambda, Nexus, etc.) have been reduced to extend gameplay.
    - **Clicker Gear**: Prices +20%, **Crit Multiplier** 5x -> 3x, and massive **Crit Chance Nerf** (HyperX 0.5%, G502 1.0%, SecretLab 1.5%).
    - **Dynamic Economy**: Prices now increase exponentially ($1.15^N$) for every item you buy. Hoarding is now expensive!
    - **Progression**: Unlocking the next tier now requires owning **10 units** of the previous item (up from 5).
    - **New Tech**: Added 3 High-Tier generators: **NVIDIA H100**, **Quantum WAN**, and **Matrioshka Brain**.
    - **Stability**: `/crypto_price` uses cached data, and the core mining loop is now **Async Optimized** (no more database lag).
- **Slots Gameplay**: Now features an **Interactive Session**. No more spam! View your session profit and spin continuously.
- **Crypto Mining**: Prices for high-end mining gear (S19, 4090) have been **drastically increased** to ensure long-term balance.
- **Cisco & AWS**: Existing generators remain unchanged.

*Happy mining!* 🚀
